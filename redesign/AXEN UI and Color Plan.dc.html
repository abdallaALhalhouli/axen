01 — The four colours

Two grounds, one ink, one signal.

Anything that isn't one of these four is a mistake. Red is a signal, not a decoration: if everything is red, nothing is.

Paper
#F3F2F2

The page. Every section sits on it. Sections are told apart by rules, not by tinted boxes.

Surface
#EAE9E9

Inputs, and the one cell you want picked out of a row — the recommended price column, the logo panel.

Ink
#201E1D

All type, the logo, the rules. Also a full field for one section only — the WhatsApp terminal, because a terminal is a screen.

Signal red
#EC3013

The WhatsApp button, section numbers, the live dot, ticks, and the closing poster band. Nothing else.

Red on paper is for interface, not paragraphs

#EC3013 on #F3F2F2 clears 3:1 — fine for buttons, icons and 12px-and-up uppercase labels. For a sentence in red, use the deep step #AE1800.

Greys come from one ramp

Body copy #444141, captions #605D5D, quiet labels #7D7979. Never a new grey mixed by eye.

Neutral 100–900 · Red 100–900 — hovers take one step, pressed states two

Arabic runs a size larger and looser in line-height than the English at the same rank — Plex Arabic's letterforms need the air. Wide letter-spacing is for Latin caps only. Never letterspace Arabic.

03 — Grid, rules, components

Structure does the decorating.

The grid

1280px max, 40px gutters. Sections are 7/5 or 5/7 splits, or 2, 3 and 4 equal cells. Cells are divided by a 1px rule, sections by a 2px rule.

Spacing

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32. Sections breathe at 80px top and bottom. No value invented between the steps.

Corners and shadows

Radius is 0 everywhere — buttons, inputs, cards, images. Nothing floats: no drop shadows on the site at all.

Buttons — the label stays flush left in a wide button

Photography — always through the grayscale filter

Screenshots and photographs print black and white, framed by a 2px rule. Never tinted, never in a rounded card, never floated on a gradient.

04 — The anti-slop rules

Why this won't read as generated.

Your list, turned into rules a future page has to pass.

Never
Neon and purple gradients
Instead

Flat fields only. One red, one ink, two papers — and the red used at most twice on a screen.

Never
Floating 3D blobs and glows
Instead

Zero shadows, zero radius. The only images are real screenshots and a real photograph of a real person.

Never
Invented statistics
Instead

A number appears only if you can prove it: 2–4 weeks, one engineer, ClinicFlow. No 99%, no “500+ clients”.

Never
Empty buzzwords
Instead

Every heading names a thing or an action. If a sentence would survive unchanged on a competitor's site, rewrite it.

Never
A button-tree chatbot demo
Instead

The demo shows a customer's real Arabic sentence being understood, priced and written to a database — with the DB line visible.

Never
One-click template look
Instead

Asymmetric 7/5 splits, one ink section, one red poster, and the logo at real size — not a centred stack of equal cards.
