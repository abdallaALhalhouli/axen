08 — Questions

The things people ask first.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, so you get a written price after a 15-minute call. That price doesn't move unless you change the scope.

How long until it's live?

Two to four weeks for most builds. ClinicFlow can be set up in days because it already exists.

Is this a chatbot with buttons?

No. No menu trees. It reads what the customer actually typed — Arabic, dialect or Arabizi — and answers, or hands the chat to you.

Who owns the work?

You do — code, repo, domain and hosting accounts in your name, handed over at the end.

Can you work with what I already have?

Usually yes. Existing site, sheets, POS or CRM — if it has an API or an export, it can be connected.

What if something breaks later?

Message on WhatsApp. Monthly support is optional; you're not charged a retainer to be answered.
